# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Workshop managers reviewing automotive work orders during the C02 hackathon.

## Product Purpose

Distinguish recorded claims from independent operational evidence, show exact
deterministic assessments and make missing evidence and the trust trail inspectable.

Lead with the work order and a connected workshop lifecycle process map. Attach
the recorded position, actual events and grouped verification issues to relevant
stages; clicking an issue opens evidence investigation. The workflow template is
product context, never supplied evidence or an invented historical timeline.

## Capabilities and Constraints

M04 renders actual persisted M02 records through the frozen M03 engine. No AI,
diagnoses, employee scoring, workflow actions, timing calculations or simulation.
Operational RLS remains enabled and forced. The local prototype uses existing
official CLI authentication for server reads; public production access is deferred.

## Brand Commitments

Workshop Flow Intelligence. Professional, light operational software with clean
blue accents, navy/slate text, restrained statuses and generous whitespace.

## Product Principles

- Recorded does not mean verified.
- Every conclusion has source references and an inspectable rule.
- Missing evidence never automatically proves conflict or operational blockage.
- Owners describe recorded assignments, never blame.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard accessible controls, text labels alongside status colors and a usable
mobile layout. Desktop/laptop review is primary.
